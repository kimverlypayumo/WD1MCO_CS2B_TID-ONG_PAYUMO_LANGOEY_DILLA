THE MAIN CHARACTERS

Protagonists

Judy Hopps

Protagonist

Judy Hopps is an optimistic rabbit who dreams of becoming a police officer. She becomes the first bunny police officer in Zootopia. Despite facing discrimination and doubt, she remains determined to prove her abilities. Her courage helps her solve the city's mystery.

Voice Actor: Ginnifer Goodwin

Nick Wilde

Protagonist

Nick Wilde is a clever fox known for his sarcastic personality and street smarts. He initially works as a small-time con artist. He later becomes Judy's partner in investigating the missing mammals case. Through their journey, Nick learns to trust others.

Voice Actor: Jason Bateman

Chief Bogo

Supporting

Chief Bogo is the head of the Zootopia Police Department. He is strict, serious, and initially doubts Judy's abilities. He eventually recognizes the importance of her investigation.

Voice Actor: Idris Elba

Gazelle

Supporting

Gazelle is a famous pop star in Zootopia. She uses her popularity to promote peace, equality, and understanding among the different animal communities.

Voice Actor: Shakira

THE OTHER SIDE OF THE STORY

Antagonists

Assistant Mayor Bellwether

Main Antagonist

Bellwether is the assistant mayor of Zootopia. She appears shy and harmless, but secretly creates conflict between predators and prey. Her actions are at the center of the mystery.

Voice Actor: Jenny Slate

Mayor Lionheart

Antagonist

Lionheart is the powerful leader of Zootopia. He makes secret decisions during the missing-mammals crisis because he wants to protect his position and reputation.

Voice Actor: J.K. Simmons

Duke Weaselton

Minor Antagonist

Duke is a small-time criminal involved in selling bootleg DVDs. His activities connect him to Judy's investigation and create complications for the heroes.

Voice Actor: Alan Tudyk

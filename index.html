About Zootopia

A World Without Limits

Zootopia is a modern mammal metropolis where animals from different habitats live together. Judy Hopps, an ambitious rabbit, becomes the first bunny police officer in the city.

When mysterious disappearances happen, Judy teams up with Nick Wilde, a clever fox. Together, they investigate a case that reveals the importance of trust, equality, and understanding.
